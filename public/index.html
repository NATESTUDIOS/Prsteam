Our Mission

Campus PRST is a collective of students from different faculties, backgrounds, and skillsets who come together with one mission: Solve real problems on campus using software and technology. We combine creativity, technical knowledge, and practical problem-solving to build solutions that genuinely help students, staff, and the entire institution.
