About Campus PRST

Campus PRST is a collective of students from different faculties, backgrounds, and skillsets who come together with one mission:

Solve real problems on campus using software and technology.

PRST isn't just a team — it's a movement inside the school where talented individuals combine creativity, technical knowledge, and practical problem-solving to build solutions that genuinely help students, staff, and the entire institution.

How Campus PRST Works

Campus PRST is divided into two specialized divisions, working together when needed:

Software Team (PRST SoftLab)

This team focuses on digital solutions.

Roles Include:

Developers (Web, Mobile, Backend)
UI/UX Designers
Data analysts
AI/ML Engineers
Cybersecurity/DevOps learners
Product managers
Technical writers

What they build:

Websites & mobile apps
Campus tools
Bots, AI assistants, automations
Platforms for departments
APIs, dashboards, management systems

Goal: Solve campus problems with software — fast, scalable, modern.

Hardware Team (PRST HardLab)

This team handles physical, engineering, and device-based solutions.

Roles Include:

Computer engineering students
Electrical/electronic engineering
Mechanical engineering
Network engineers
IoT/robotics enthusiasts

What they build:

Smart devices
Embedded systems (Arduino, Raspberry Pi)
Campus utilities
Robotics projects
Hardware-software integrated solutions

Goal: Turn physical problems into innovative tech-enabled products.
